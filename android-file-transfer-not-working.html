Android File Transfer isn't working on your Mac?

You're not doing anything wrong. The app is abandoned. Google quietly pulled Android File Transfer's download link from android.com back in early 2024, and that page now points to a Windows-only app instead. The tool itself never got another update, so it fails more and more on current Macs.

What's actually going on

There was never a formal "discontinued" notice, which is half of why this is so confusing. But the download is gone, the only replacement Google points you to (Quick Share) is Windows-only, and the old app chokes on Apple Silicon and recent macOS. The symptoms are always the same:

  1. "Could not connect to the device," over and over, when the connection drops or never opens.
  2. A hard 4 GB limit per file, so one large video just refuses to copy.
  3. It works once, then quits after the phone sleeps, or after the next macOS update.

Quick things to try first

  1. Use a cable that carries data, not a charge-only one. If the phone charges but never shows up, swap the cable first.
  2. On the phone, pull down the notification shade and tap File Transfer (it's sometimes labelled "MTP" or "Transferring files").
  3. Unplug, replug, and try a different USB port.

If that fixes it, great. If it works once and dies the next time, or never opens at all, the problem is the app, not your setup.

The simpler fix: skip the broken app

Comprador shows your phone in Finder like a USB drive. There's no separate window to babysit, no kernel extension, and no Developer Options to dig through. It's free and open source.

  1. Plug your phone in with a data cable.
  2. Pull down the notification shade and tap File Transfer.
  3. Your phone appears in Finder's sidebar under Locations. Drag files in and out, like any other volume.

Questions

Is Android File Transfer discontinued?

No formal notice, but Google removed the download in early 2024 and the page now serves a Windows-only app. It's unmaintained and increasingly fails on Apple Silicon and recent macOS.

Why won't my Android phone show up on my Mac?

Usually a charge-only cable, the phone not set to File Transfer mode, or the transfer app being broken. The first two take a second to check; the third is Android File Transfer's own fault.

Does Comprador need Developer Options or USB debugging?

No. You never touch Developer Options. Plug in, tap File Transfer, done.

Does it work on Apple Silicon?

Yes. It's a native app, signed and notarized by Apple, for macOS 13 or later.

Is it safe?

Free and open source, no telemetry. It talks to your phone over USB and serves the files to Finder over a loopback connection the network can't reach. Nothing leaves your Mac.
