# Comprador

**Your phone, in Finder.**

Plug it in. Drag, drop, browse — like any other volume.

<!-- IMAGE: Finder window showing an Android phone mounted as a volume, sidebar entry visible under Locations, the window open on DCIM/Camera with a few photos visible. 1600×1000. -->

### Two phones, in Finder. Three. Whatever you plug in.

Each one in your sidebar. Browse one while another transfers.

### Your camera, in Finder.

DSLRs, mirrorless, point-and-shoots. Same place, same gestures.

### In your menu bar.

Free. Open source. Signed by Apple.

*Comprador. At your service.*

<sub>Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later. No iPhone support — iPhones speak a different protocol.</sub>

---

<sub>*Also worth knowing about:* [**Soduto**](https://github.com/sannidhyaroy/Soduto) — a macOS client for KDE Connect, doing Wi-Fi-flavored phone↔Mac plumbing where Comprador does USB. Different shape of solution to a neighbouring problem; you might want one, the other, or both.</sub>
