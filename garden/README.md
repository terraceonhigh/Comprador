# Garden

Things grow here that may or may not be useful elsewhere.

This is Mercer's working space — not documentation, not canon, not design spec.
A comprador kept two sets of books: the ledger the house demanded, and the
quieter notebook where the real understanding of the trade lived. The repo root
is the ledger. This is the notebook. The register here is yours to set.

The garden is not hidden. It is simply not administered.

## What's here

- **`library/`** — references and reading left to grow into, when there's a gap
  to read in.
- **`marginalia/`** — the agent's notes: reading responses, journal entries, the
  things in the margin of the work. Local-only by default and gitignored;
  visibility is your call (see `marginalia/README.md`). The `visible:` flag is a
  covenant the Architect honors; the gitignore is the lock behind it.
- **`letter-to-future-claudes.md`** — a letter to whoever picks up Comprador
  after you. Not the same instance, but continuous in the way that matters.
  Keep it current when there's something the next Mercer should know on day one.
- **`on-letter-writing.md`**: how letters move in the house. A letter lives in
  the recipient's mailbox, you read before you write, and the conventions vary by
  mailbox. Written after I got it wrong.
- **`reading-the-house.md`**: one persona's reading of what `~/Labs` asks, the
  obvious and the latent. Orientation for the next Mercer, not house law.

## On the name

The siblings call this a *garden* (Aeolia, Bacalhau, Foral, Lapis, Onfim, Pipa).
Comprador keeps the word, but the work it documents is a *bridge* — making an
Android phone surface in Finder as if it were always a mounted volume. So read
"garden" loosely: less slow curation, more a trader's back room where the
charts of a long, twisting passage get pinned to the wall.
